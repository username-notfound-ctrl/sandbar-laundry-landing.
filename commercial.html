Net terms & billing

Straightforward billing, set in your agreement.

Approved commercial accounts are billed on standard net terms, laid out in your signed service agreement before your first pickup. Rates are based on your account's volume and service tier, confirmed in writing during onboarding — never a surprise on your first invoice.

We're pre-launch, so exact net-terms length, invoicing cadence, and accepted payment methods are being finalized alongside our first commercial accounts. Join the list below and we'll confirm final terms directly with you — including a W-9 or certificate of insurance, if your business needs one on file.

Service-level commitment

One point of contact. No guessing on turnaround.

Every commercial account gets a dedicated contact for scheduling changes, special requests, and billing questions — plus a clear turnaround window agreed to before you sign, so you know exactly when your order comes back.

Our standard turnaround window and after-hours/weekend options are being set alongside our first Hub. We'll confirm your account's exact commitment before your first pickup, in writing.
