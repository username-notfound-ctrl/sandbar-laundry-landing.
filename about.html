Our story

A local business, built for this coastline.

Sandbar Laundry Co. started with a simple frustration: laundry takes up hours nobody in Jacksonville has to spare.

Why we started

Onslow County deserves better than a laundromat run-around.

Between work, family, and everything else, laundry is the chore that never quite gets done right. Sandbar Laundry Co. exists to take it off your plate entirely — drop off a bag, get back to your day, pick up clean laundry when it's ready.

We're building this as a genuinely local company: founded in Jacksonville, NC, operating out of a Jacksonville facility, for the neighbors who live here.

Sandbar Laundry Co, LLC

A North Carolina limited liability company, headquartered in Jacksonville, NC.

What we stand for

The principles behind Sandbar

Veteran-owned

Sandbar Laundry Co. is founded by a disabled veteran, and that shows up in how we treat the military and veteran families who call Onslow County home — including a standing discount on every plan.

Eco-minded, on purpose

Sustainability is a deliberate part of how we operate: efficient equipment, eco-friendly detergents, and routes designed to minimize unnecessary driving — not a line added after the fact.

Honest, even pre-launch

We'd rather tell you we're still building than oversell what isn't ready yet. What you see on this site is where we actually are.
