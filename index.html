Rooted in Onslow County

A coastal Carolina business, built for this community.

Sandbar Laundry Co. is a Jacksonville, NC company from the ground up — not a franchise, not an app dressed up as a local business. We're building this for our neighbors, including the military and veteran families who make up so much of this town.

Sustainability is part of how we operate, not a marketing line: efficient equipment, eco-friendly detergents, and a route model built to cut down on unnecessary driving.
