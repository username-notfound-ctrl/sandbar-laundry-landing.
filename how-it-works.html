The process

From hamper to folded, in four steps.

No apps to configure, no machines to babysit. Here's exactly what happens between dropping off a full bag and picking up a clean one.

01

Get your Sandbar bag

Once you sign up, you'll get a reusable, labeled Sandbar laundry bag — this is what travels between your home and your Hub every time.

02

Drop it at your Hub

Every neighborhood Hub is open on your schedule — no waiting around for a truck or booking a pickup window. Drop your bag off whenever it's convenient, day or night.

03

We wash, dry, and fold

Your laundry heads to our Jacksonville facility, where it's washed and dried with eco-friendly products and folded by hand — never mixed with anyone else's load.

04

Pick up when you're notified

We'll text you the moment your bag is back at the Hub, clean and folded. Swing by whenever works — no rush, no line.

Coming later

A doorstep tier, for whenever you need it
